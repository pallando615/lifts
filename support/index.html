Lifts: Strength Log Support

Lifts is a strength workout log for iPhone. It needs iOS 26 or later.

Need a hand? Write to asendlabs@gmail.com and say which iPhone and iOS version you use. I read everything.

My Apple Watch workout isn't showing up

Lifts shows the duration, calories and heart rate of a Strength Training, Functional Strength Training or Core Training workout from Apple Health. Check that you connected Apple Health (Settings in Lifts, Fitness) and that the workout overlaps the time you logged your sets. Open the workout in History and tap Look Again. A workout from your Watch can take a few minutes to reach your iPhone.

I finished a workout by mistake

In History, open the workout, tap the ⋯ menu and choose Resume Workout. It works for 24 hours after you finish.

Sync between my devices

Sign in to iCloud on each device and keep Sync with iCloud on in Lifts under Settings. After you switch it, restart Lifts. Units, weight increment and accent color stay on each device.

Backing up or moving to a new iPhone

With iCloud sync on, your library follows you to a new iPhone. The calories and heart rate from Fitness don't sync: they stay on the iPhone where they were saved. To bring them along, open Settings, then Backup & Restore, and choose Save Backup, then restore that file on the new iPhone. Otherwise Lifts looks up the last 14 days of workouts in Health again. A backup is also a copy you control. To get your data into a spreadsheet, choose Export to CSV in Settings.

Deleting my data

Lifts keeps your data on your iPhone and, if sync is on, in your private iCloud. Delete workouts in History, or delete the app. The Privacy Policy explains how to remove what is in iCloud.
